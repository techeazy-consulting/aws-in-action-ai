Password reset policy

Every employee can reset their own password. Nobody on the IT team will ever ask for it.

How to reset

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password".
  2. Enter your work email address. A reset code is sent to your registered phone.
  3. Enter the code and choose a new password.

Password rules

  • At least 14 characters, with no part of your name or email address.
  • It must differ from your last five passwords.
  • After five wrong attempts the account is locked until the IT team unlocks it.

If the reset code does not arrive

Contact the IT service desk. They will check your identity before resetting anything.
