VPN access policy

Staff working away from the office connect to company systems through the VPN client.

Getting connected

  1. Install the VPN client from the company software portal.
  2. Sign in with your work email address and approve the prompt on your registered phone.
  3. Wait for the client to show "Connected" before opening internal tools.

If the VPN will not connect

  1. Quit the VPN client and start it again.
  2. Check that your device clock is set automatically.
  3. Switch to a different network, for example a phone hotspot, and try once more.
  4. If it still fails, contact the IT service desk and say which step failed.

Rules

  • Use the VPN on any public or shared network.
  • Do not share your VPN sign-in or approve a prompt you did not start.
