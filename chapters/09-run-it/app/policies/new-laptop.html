New laptop and equipment policy

Company laptops are issued by the IT team and remain company property.

Requesting a laptop

  1. Your manager submits an equipment request that names you and your role.
  2. The IT team prepares the laptop with the standard software and disk encryption.
  3. You collect it in person and sign for it.

Lost or stolen equipment

Report it to the IT service desk straight away so the device can be locked and wiped.

Returning equipment

Return the laptop, charger and any accessories to the IT team when you leave or are issued a replacement.
